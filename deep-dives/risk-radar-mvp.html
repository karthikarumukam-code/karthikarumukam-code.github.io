Problem statement

A Project Manager's risk signals are scattered across separate documents — status reports, RAID logs, meeting minutes — so a compounding risk often isn't noticed until it's too late to do anything but escalate or absorb the overrun.

Use case

An IT Project Manager loads their own project's status report, RAID log, dependency register, project plan, and meeting minutes. The tool cross-checks these documents for risk patterns — compounding risks, Critical/High risks, and newly raised risks — and flags them immediately, with every flag traced back to its source, so the PM can act and update stakeholders before it becomes escalation-only.

MVP — built
  • Single-project dashboard: status report, RAID log, dependency register, project plan, meeting minutes
  • Rule-based extraction — deterministic, offline, no API key needed
  • LLM extraction (Anthropic / OpenRouter / Ollama) compared against the baseline
  • Risk correlation — compounding risk flagged when signals span multiple categories
  • Overdue project-plan tasks flagged as schedule risk
  • "New since your last report" 🆕 flagging
  • Deterministic scoring — Probability × Impact × Urgency, with a severity band
  • Evidence trail — every flag links back to its exact source
  • PM's own RAG shown next to the AI's read, disagreement surfaced, not hidden
  • Trend view across historical weekly reports
  • Upload Word, PDF, PPTX, or email files, not just plain text
  • Portfolio Overview — all projects side by side
  • Test suite proving it catches what it should — 65 tests, 11/11 recall
Not in scope
  • No connection to Jira, Azure DevOps, or ServiceNow
  • No live monitoring — only checks when you run it
  • No custom-trained AI model, just a formula and rules
  • No automated actions — it flags, a human decides
  • No real multi-project portfolio management
  • No privacy/security design for real documents naming real people
  • No way for a flagged team to contest a flag
  • RAID log, dependency register, and plan still need to be CSV files
  • Outlook .msg files aren't supported, only .eml

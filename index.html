How I work on AI projects

Three habits I carried from program delivery into AI work, each visible in the case studies.

01

Scope honestly

Every case study ends with its limits: synthetic data, estimates that were never measured, a demo that is only an illustration. A claim is only as strong as its evidence.

See: Risk Radar, Aurelia
02

Measure against a baseline

A rule-based baseline before the LLM. A blind guess before the model. A pass/fail threshold set before training the challenger. Improvement means something only against a fair comparison.

See: Risk Radar, Test-Bench
03

Keep the human decision human

The Risk Radar never acts on its own: it shows the evidence and the PM decides. The feasibility study put works-council clearance and an escalation safety test in front of any AI build.

See: Risk Radar, Aurelia
